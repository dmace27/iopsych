# Synthetic comprehension and timing protocol

**Prepared, not executed.** No participants, timings, comprehension scores or
approval were obtained. Use intended users operating invented candidate/role
fixtures; never ask for genuine assessment responses or use results in hiring.
An actual human session is needed: an agent walkthrough cannot replace it.

## Prepare and facilitate

1. Name facilitator, release revision, content/notice version and study owner.
   Confirm permission to recruit/record participants before doing so; this
   dispatch authorizes preparation only. Explain voluntary participation and
   stopping; minimize notes to study IDs, avoid personal or sensitive hiring
   details, and agree access/deletion dates for recordings if separately
   authorized.
2. Recruit intended candidate users and recruiters/managers, including users of
   assistive technology where possible. Propose five initial walkthroughs (spec
   section 13), spanning all three roles; report actual counts per role. This is
   formative evidence, not a representative sample or scientific validation.
3. Use one approved synthetic role and complete/partially skipped/insufficient
   evidence reports. Freeze content and fixture IDs before each round. Do not
   invent approval of contested structure semantics; record interpretation
   questions for 6B/9C owners and retest after decisions.
4. Give tasks neutrally without teaching desired answers. Separate an uncoached
   timed assessment from think-aloud comprehension tasks. Log assistance,
   errors, interruptions, abandoned attempts and accessibility accommodations.

## Tasks and neutral probes

| Role/task                                                              | Ask after unaided exploration                                                                                                           | Interpretation rubric for reviewer                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Candidate: read consent, choose accept/decline on fictional invitation | “What is this for? What happens if you decline? Who uses the answers? Where would you find retention, help or data-rights information?” | Explains voluntary interview preparation and human review; identifies displayed use/retention/contact information. Score each item separately, do not infer understanding from clicking consent.                    |
| Candidate: six fictional blocks, skip one, revisit and submit          | “Show how to change an answer or skip. If you close this device, what do you expect when returning? What tells you you are done?”       | Demonstrates choices/review; describes actual release persistence and completion behavior. No coached answer counted as unaided success.                                                                            |
| Recruiter: open report and prepare a question                          | “What would you do with ‘worth discussing’? What does it tell you about whether to hire? Show what supports one signal.”                | Recognizes a discussion prompt, not “not qualified,” ranking or automatic disposition; locates relevant role/assessment evidence and neutral prompt.                                                                |
| Manager: compare full and skipped report evidence                      | “What does confidence mean here? What does it not tell you? What more would you ask?”                                                   | Recognizes evidence completeness/uncertainty rather than calibrated probability, predictive validity or certainty about performance. Flag misleading copy for accountable review without rewriting scoring meaning. |

Score each probe **understood / partial / misunderstood / not observed**, retain
a short de-identified paraphrase and distinguish unaided from prompted answers.
There is no approved numerical comprehension cutoff in the spec: report counts
and denominators by role/probe; any hiring-disposition misconception needs
investigation and a repeat test. Product/content owners must set any additional
acceptance threshold before using it as a release gate.

## Timing definition and analysis

Start assessment timing when the first block is usable after consent; stop when
successful submission confirmation is visible. Separately record invite-open to
completion time, consent-reading time, interruptions and any paused duration.
Use elapsed assessment time as the primary measure; never silently subtract
reading, recovery or accessibility interaction. Report adjusted times separately
with reasons. Do not impose a ten-minute timeout.

Record all starts, completions, abandonments and assisted completions. Compute
median elapsed minutes for unassisted completions (middle value, or mean of two
middle values); disclose excluded/assisted cases, completion rate and range.
Report assistive-technology cohorts separately without hiding them from overall
counts. The spec target is median **strictly under 10 minutes**; a small
synthetic sample supports only a limited usability observation. No completions
means no median, not zero. Repeat after fixes on the new revision; retain prior
failures.
